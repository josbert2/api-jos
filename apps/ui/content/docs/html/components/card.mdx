Cards group related content and actions in a single container. They support borders, side layouts, sizes, and header/footer slots.

<CardClassesTable />

## Default Card

Use `card` plus a `card-body` for the main content. Add `card-title` and `card-actions` for structure.

<HtmlPreview name="card-default" />

## Bordered Card

Apply `card-border` to add a visible border around the card.

<HtmlPreview name="card-bordered" />

## Card with Image

Place a `<figure>` as the first child to act as the card header. The corners inherit from the card's radius.

<HtmlPreview name="card-image" />

## Side Card

Use `card-side` to lay out the figure beside the body for horizontal cards.

<HtmlPreview name="card-side" />

## Card Sizes

Control the padding density using `card-xs`, `card-sm`, `card-md` (default), `card-lg`, or `card-xl`.

<HtmlPreview name="card-sizes" />

## Card with Header and Footer

Compose `card-header`, `card-body`, and `card-footer` to organize content into sections.
