Indicators position a small element on top of another element. Use them to attach badges, counts, or status dots to buttons, cards, or avatars.

<IndicatorClassesTable />

## Default Indicator

Wrap the target in `indicator` and add the floating element with `indicator-item`. By default it sits on the top-end corner.

<HtmlPreview name="indicator-default" />

## Indicator Positions

Combine `indicator-top`, `indicator-middle`, `indicator-bottom` with `indicator-start`, `indicator-center`, or `indicator-end` to choose any corner.

<HtmlPreview name="indicator-positions" />

## Indicator on Buttons

Pin badges to buttons to show counts, statuses, or alerts.

<HtmlPreview name="indicator-button" />

## Indicator on Cards
