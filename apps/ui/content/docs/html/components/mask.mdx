---
title: Mask
description: Masks crop any element into a geometric shape.
---

Masks crop the content of an element into a predefined shape via CSS `mask-image`. They work with solid backgrounds, gradients, or `<img>` tags.

<MaskClassesTable />

## Default

Apply `mask` together with a shape modifier such as `mask-squircle`, `mask-circle`, `mask-square` or `mask-hexagon`.

<HtmlPreview name="mask-default" />

## Geometric Shapes

A wide range of geometric shapes is available out of the box.

<HtmlPreview name="mask-shapes" />

## Stars and Heart

Use `mask-star`, `mask-star-2` and `mask-heart` for decorative shapes.

<HtmlPreview name="mask-stars" />

## With Images

Masks crop any element including `<img>` tags.

<HtmlPreview name="mask-image" />

## Half Masks

Use `mask-half-1` and `mask-half-2` to render only one half of a shape.
