---
title: Swap
description: Swap toggles between two elements based on a checkbox.
---

Swap shows one of two children based on whether the inner checkbox is checked. Add `swap-rotate` or `swap-flip` modifiers for animated transitions.

<SwapClassesTable />

## Default

The base swap fades between `swap-on` and `swap-off` content.

<HtmlPreview name="swap-default" />

## Rotate

`swap-rotate` rotates the children when toggled, ideal for theme or play/pause buttons.

<HtmlPreview name="swap-rotate" />

## Flip

`swap-flip` adds a 3D flip transition between the two states.

<HtmlPreview name="swap-flip" />
